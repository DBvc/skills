# Conditional evidence checks

Load the applicable section, not this entire document as a mandatory checklist.
These are inquiry controls, not a taxonomy of people or a universal scoring model.

## Evidence provenance and trust

For a pivotal empirical claim, establish what was actually observed, how the observation was generated, and which portion supports the proposed inference.
A direct source helps attribution, but is not automatically methodologically sound. A careful synthesis may be more informative than one weak primary study. Match authority to the question; institutional prestige, official status, credentials, and majority agreement are not substitutes for checking scope and method.

Identify the relevant population, sampling frame, date, operational definition, denominator, comparator, and any material uncertainty. Do not demand every field for a claim that does not need them.

Follow source lineage when dependence could matter. Several articles quoting the same study count as one underlying study for that claim, while additional independently collected evidence can add support. Shared ideology alone neither proves dependence nor discredits a finding. Describe independence as unknown when it cannot be established.

Record only useful coverage: what material was inspected, whether coverage is partial, and the limitation. A search snippet is a lead, not proof of having read the underlying source. A failed URL does not justify reconstructing its content from memory or its title.

Sources may be excluded for demonstrated unreliability or irrelevant scope. Do not demand universal trust. When the argument automatically discredits every possible challenger, inspect whether the exclusion rule is independently testable and applied consistently to favored sources. Ask what admissible observation could distinguish genuine misconduct from an inconvenient result. Do not diagnose indoctrination or force trust.

## Descriptive claims and scope

A real example establishes possibility, not prevalence. Missing denominators, self-selection, survivorship, platform selection, and time/place mismatch can limit generalization. Name only biases that the available collection process supports.

An individual counterexample refutes a literal universal, but not a claim that something is common or becoming more likely. Preserve "all", "most", "some", "increasing", and "relative to what". Report a defensible weaker claim separately from the original.

A source sample can be diverse in publishers yet homogeneous in evidence generation. Conversely, independent methods can support the same conclusion without forming an echo chamber. Do not optimize diversity for its own sake.

## Causal claims

Ask which comparison would isolate the proposed cause rather than merely co-occurring change. Check plausible common causes, reverse direction, selection, changed measurement, and timing when they matter.

A useful alternative must explain relevant observations and imply a discriminating check. Do not generate a fixed number of rival stories. A randomized result within one setting may support an average causal effect there without proving external applicability or universal benefit.

A before/after trend alone is usually insufficient to isolate an intervention. A trend moving in the favorable direction does not refute harm relative to a counterfactual that would have improved faster. State the identification limit, not just "correlation is not causation".

Aggregate and subgroup comparisons can disagree. Inspect whether the target is composition, within-group performance, or a total outcome; do not privilege an aggregate or subgroup statistic without the corresponding question.

## Predictions

Clarify event, population, horizon, baseline, and resolution criterion before giving a scored probability. Separate "will happen", "could happen", and "happens more often than otherwise".

A future observation is useful only if it actually distinguishes the forecast from plausible alternatives. Raw job growth is not by itself a refutation of an AI-related causal reduction in employment relative to the no-AI path.

Subjective probability may be appropriate for a clearly defined forecast, but must be labeled and tied to assumptions/base rates when available. Do not assign percentages to a vague worldview, moral preference, personal bias, or general credibility.

## Values and experience

Empirical evidence can inform a value judgment's factual premises and consequences; it does not uniquely choose values. Make conflicts and trade-offs explicit without treating the agent's preferences as neutral truth.

A preference such as "I enjoy quiet evenings" is not an empirical generalization needing counterevidence. "Everyone would be better off avoiding social contact" introduces factual and normative claims that can be examined separately.

Treat reported feelings and experiences as reported experiences. Distinguish them from inferences about another person's motives or an entire group. A limitation in causal evidence does not erase distress or imply that the reported event did not happen.

For violence, abuse, urgent illness, or immediate danger, prioritize safe protective action and domain guidance over an abstract demand for balanced accounts. A skill cannot authorize professional certainty or covert persuasion.

## Search and framing

A neutral initial query is a way to reduce loaded wording, not a promise of neutral search results.
Target an actual uncertainty: prevalence needs a denominator; causal attribution needs a comparator; apparently broad agreement may need source-lineage checking.

Look for the strongest relevant credible challenge when the conclusion is contested. Distinguish a substantiated challenge, a speculative alternative, and a known refuted assertion. Do not amplify a harmful or false claim just to represent "both sides".

No fixed language, country, platform, or source quota. Cross-language or cross-domain search is justified when the subject's evidence is likely to lie there, not as decoration. Do not turn translated coverage of one source into independent confirmation.

If query results repeat one frame, inspect how the underlying data were collected and consider a materially different question or missing hypothesis. The agent's framing is also fallible. A brief coverage sweep before choosing a focal check prevents locking onto the first convenient objection.

## When another check is worth doing

Choose another check when it can realistically alter the conclusion, warranted scope, strength, or safe next action at reasonable cost. This is a qualitative prioritization rule, not a computable information-gain guarantee.

For a contested case, do not stop solely because supporting results are easy to find. Consider the strongest identified limitation or challenge, and disclose material unexplored territory.

Stop with a bounded result when access/budget is exhausted, remaining sources only duplicate inspected material, or the unresolved gap cannot presently be resolved. Preserve uncertainty instead of seeking a convenient answer. Genuine unresolved disagreement can be a complete audit result.

An update condition should connect evidence to the claim: "an independent representative sample measuring X would strengthen/weaken this population claim" is better than "read more perspectives". A fully settled arithmetic or deductive question needs no invented uncertainty.
