# dbx-product-conception

A DBX skill for creating and selecting product directions before product correctness can be judged.

## What it does

- turns weak signals, enabling changes, human tensions, and maker conviction into product opportunities;
- generates structurally different product logics rather than feature variants;
- makes concepts comparable through concrete experience specimens;
- performs prototype-driven creative selection;
- commits a product thesis, boundary, non-goals, kill list, and proof handoff;
- separates evidence, inference, conviction, bets, and unknowns;
- supports long-term taste calibration through prediction and outcome review.

## Boundary

Use `dbx-product-conception` when the product itself is not yet defined or needs category-level reframing.

Use `dbx-product-judgment` when a defined concept, feature, PRD, live product, roadmap, or competitor position exists and the primary question is whether it is product-correct.

Use `dbx-crystallize` after the direction is selected and the work becomes requirements, scope, edge cases, acceptance criteria, and implementation handoff.

The skill is informed by source-grounded study of Steve Jobs and Apple product creation, but it does not impersonate Jobs or promise his tacit taste. Its transferable unit is the process: broad inputs, structural insight, concrete making, creative selection, focus, and consequence-based feedback.

## Report validation

```bash
python3 skills/dbx-product-conception/scripts/validate-conception-report.py path/to/report.md
```

This checks report structure and epistemic labels. It does not prove originality or product quality.
